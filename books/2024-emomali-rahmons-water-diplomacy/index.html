From the first step to global partnership. Documents, speeches and materials. 1999–2024

Publisher page

Also published as Водная дипломатия Эмомали Рахмона (Russian, 2024, Ves' Mir; ISBN 9785777709462).

For the first time all the main documents of the UN General Assembly and speeches by the President of the Republic of Tajikistan Emomali Rahmon devoted to water resources issues and the problem of glacier conservation are published in one volume. Documents and speeches are vivid historical materials that reveal the history of the emergence and development of water diplomacy of Emomali Rahmon, which has become a new phenomenon in international relations. The publication opens with a Preface by Sirojiddin Muhriddin, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Tajikistan, which provides a deep and reasoned analysis of the origin and expansion of international support from the community of nations for the President of the Republic of Tajikistan Emomali Rahmon’s water and climate initiatives. The book includes speeches by the President of the Republic of Tajikistan Emomali Rahmon, resolutions of the UN General Assembly, major declarations and reports of the UN Secretary-General on water initiatives of the Republic of Tajikistan. This publication is addressed to a wide range of readers interested in contemporary international relations.
